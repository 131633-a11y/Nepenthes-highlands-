Nepenthes Highlands

A large misty ridge thick with pitcher plants. Chop trees, craft tools, collect one of each of the forty species, then propagate them. You begin in a small greenhouse (expand it up to ginormous) with 21 planters, a 100-block peat bed, mother plants and a trading post (R on the stand) where you sell plants and seeds for money and buy growth upgrades. Mythic hybrids (about 0.1% of plants and seeds, likelier when two species flower side by side) sell for a fortune. Peat blocks are beds too: place peat, then sow into it. H returns home.

WASD move · Space jump · ←↑↓→ look · Shift sprint · E dig up a plant (wild or in a planter) to move it · C inventory and 2×2 crafting · 1–9 picks an item · ZX cycle
Hold F to break a block (stone is slow without a pickaxe). R places a block, or uses a crafting table or planter. T forces placing. In menus, arrow keys move, Enter selects, Esc closes. No mouse needed.
